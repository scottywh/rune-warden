RUNE WARDEN

The Sky Span is shattered. The Hollow King broke it, and the beacons of the ten realms have gone dark. You are Kari, keeper of the last beacon. Old Yrsa's last words were simple: relight them all.

Move: left-side stick or WASD / arrows.
Attack: rune button or Space — Kari casts blue rune magic now, same damage as the old sword.
3D is the default view. CAM cycles chase / iso / first-person; drag the right side to orbit, Q/E rotate. The 3D button falls back to the 2D view.
CMD button: command mode — tap a hero to select, tap ground to move them, tap a foe to attack. Folk you meet join your party. SWITCH button: play as the next hero in your party, single player — one keeper cannot carry three Sigils alone.
Reach the glowing beacon in each realm. Talk to folk you meet; some will help. Hrafn the raven scouts ahead.
