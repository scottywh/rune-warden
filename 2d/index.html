RUNE WARDEN

The Sky Span is shattered. The Hollow King broke it, and the beacons of the six realms have gone dark. You are Kari, keeper of the last beacon. Old Yrsa's last words were simple: relight them all.

Move: left-side stick or WASD / arrows.
Attack: sword button or Space.
View: ISO/TOP button switches between the simulated-3D view and top-down.
CMD button: command mode — tap a hero to select, tap ground to move them, tap a foe to attack. Folk you meet in command mode join your party.
Reach the glowing beacon in each realm. Talk to folk you meet; some will help. Hrafn the raven scouts ahead.

v1.9.0 — vision hero: blue steel armor, helmet, rune staff casting blue magic, stone arch + rune pedestal at the beacon, moss, bigger crystals and chests. Gameplay unchanged underneath.

An original tale in the spirit of the old Norse dungeon crawls. No gods were copied; the Hollow King is ours alone.
